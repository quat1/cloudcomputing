# Ad libraries: where to find competitors' ads and what each one shows

Most of these are JavaScript applications. A plain fetch often returns an empty shell. Try the
fetch, then a browser automation tool if one is available in the session, then ask the user for
screenshots or an export. Say which route you used.

## Meta Ad Library (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network)

- URL: `https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=all&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=<competitor>`
  or search by Page once you know the Page ID.
- Shows: active and (for some regions and ad categories) inactive ads, start date, platforms,
  all creative variations, the CTA button, and the landing URL. For EU audiences it also shows
  reach and targeting summaries.
- Filters worth using: active only, media type (image/video), country, date range.
- Signal: "Started running on <date>" with status Active. Many near-identical versions means
  they are testing; one long-running version means they found a winner.
- No login needed for basic viewing. Reach and audience data is EU-only.

## Google Ads Transparency Center (Search, YouTube, Display, Shopping)

- URL: `https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=anywhere&domain=<competitor-domain>`
- Shows: ads by advertiser, format (text, image, video), date range the ad was shown, regions.
  Search ads show the headline and description combinations.
- Signal: date range length. Text ad variations reveal their headline tests.
- Also search the competitor's brand and category terms on Google and note which ads appear.

## TikTok Creative Center / Top Ads

- URL: `https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/pc/en`
- Shows: top-performing ads by region, industry, and objective, with likes, CTR bands, and the
  video itself. You cannot always search by advertiser; search by keyword and industry.
- Signal: TikTok ranks these by performance, so anything listed is above average by definition.
- Transcribe the first 3 seconds of a video verbatim; that is the hook.

## LinkedIn Ad Library

- URL: `https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/search?companyIds=<id>` or search by company name
  at `https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/home`
- Shows: sponsored content from the last year, with dates and (for EU) impressions by country.
- B2B niches only. Lead-gen forms and document ads are common formats here.

## YouTube

- Google Ads Transparency Center covers YouTube video ads. Also search
  `site:youtube.com "<competitor>"` for the brand channel; ads are often uploaded there as
  unlisted or public videos.

## X (Twitter), Pinterest, Snapchat, Reddit

- X ads repository: `https://ads.x.com/transparency` (limited; EU-focused).
- Pinterest: no public library; rely on screenshots.
- Snapchat: `https://adsgallery.snap.com/` (EU political/issue ads mainly).
- Reddit: no public library; search the competitor's name with "promoted".

## Third-party and secondhand sources (label as such)

- Creator and agency breakdowns: search `"<competitor>" ads breakdown`, `"<competitor>" ad
  creative`, `"<competitor>" "ad library"` with the current month and year.
- Newsletters and swipe-file sites that cover the niche.
- Competitor landing pages, which show the offer and the promise the ads point to.

## Recording the snapshot

Note the date and time of each library visit in the report. Libraries change daily; a reader
needs to know when the picture was taken.
