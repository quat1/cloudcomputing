# Taxonomy: hooks, offers, formats, angles, awareness

Use these labels in the breakdown table so patterns can be counted. Add a label when nothing
fits, but prefer reusing one.

## Hooks (the first line or first 3 seconds)

| Label | Pattern | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Callout | Names the audience | "Shopify sellers doing over $50k/mo:" |
| Pain | Names the problem in the viewer's words | "Still reconciling Amazon payouts by hand?" |
| Question | Asks something the viewer wants answered | "What's the real cost of a bad bookkeeper?" |
| Bold claim | Specific, surprising result | "We cut their close from 12 days to 2." |
| Number | Leads with a figure | "3 tax deductions ecommerce sellers miss" |
| Curiosity gap | Withholds the payoff | "The spreadsheet mistake that triggered an audit" |
| Social proof | Leads with others' results | "Trusted by 2,000 DTC brands" |
| Contrarian | Challenges the common belief | "Stop hiring a bookkeeper. Do this instead." |
| Before / after | Shows the transformation | Side-by-side, time-lapse |
| POV / story | First-person scenario | "POV: it's April 10 and your books aren't done" |
| Demonstration | Shows the product doing the thing | Screen recording with a result on screen |
| Offer-first | Leads with the deal | "First month free, no contract" |
| Native / pattern interrupt | Looks like organic content, a text message, a note | Handwritten, lo-fi, meme |

## Offers (what is exchanged)

| Label | Shape |
|---|---|
| Free trial | time-boxed or usage-boxed, with or without card |
| Freemium | free tier forever |
| Lead magnet | guide, template, calculator, checklist, webinar |
| Discount | percent, amount, first-month, annual |
| Bundle / bonus stack | product plus extras, usually with a value anchor |
| Guarantee | money-back, results-based, "or we work for free" |
| Demo / call | book time with a human |
| Audit / assessment | free review of the prospect's situation |
| Quiz / diagnostic | self-assessment that ends in a recommendation |
| Urgency / scarcity | deadline, limited seats, price rising |
| Price anchor | "less than a coffee a day", versus the cost of the problem |
| Done-for-you | migration, setup, onboarding included |
| No offer | pure brand or awareness |

## Formats

Static image · Carousel · Short video (under 30s) · Long video · UGC testimonial · Talking head
/ founder · Screen recording / product demo · Meme or native-looking · Text-only (search, X) ·
Comparison table · Document / PDF ad (LinkedIn) · Lead form ad · Collection / catalog ·
Reel/TikTok vertical · Interactive (quiz, poll)

## Angles (the core argument)

Save time · Save money / avoid cost · Make money · Reduce risk / avoid pain · Status and
identity ("for serious operators") · Simplicity / ease · Speed · Authority / expertise ·
Belonging / community · Fear of missing out · Fear of doing it wrong · Trust and transparency ·
Fun / delight

## Awareness levels (Eugene Schwartz)

| Level | Viewer's state | Hooks that fit |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | does not know they have the problem | story, pattern interrupt, contrarian |
| Problem-aware | feels the pain, no solution in mind | pain, question, callout |
| Solution-aware | knows the category, not the brand | comparison, bold claim, demonstration |
| Product-aware | knows the brand, not convinced | social proof, guarantee, offer |
| Most-aware | ready to buy | offer-first, urgency, price |

## Proof elements

Specific numbers · Customer logos · Named testimonials · Star ratings and review counts ·
Press mentions · Awards and certifications · Live demonstration · Founder credibility ·
Case study result · "As seen on" · Guarantees (proof of confidence)

## CTA buttons (and what they imply)

Learn more (soft, awareness) · Sign up / Get started (trial) · Shop now (ecommerce) · Book now
(service) · Download (lead magnet) · Get offer (promo) · Contact us (B2B) · Apply now ·
Subscribe · Watch more

## Signal strength

- **Run length**: under 14 days = testing; 14 to 60 = promising; 60+ = likely working;
  180+ = a workhorse.
- **Variations**: many near-identical copies = they are optimizing this concept.
- **Cross-platform**: the same concept on two platforms = they trust it.
